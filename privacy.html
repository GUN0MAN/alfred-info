Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Data accessed. With the user's permission, Alfred accesses Gmail and Google Calendar data.

Use. This data is used only to provide Alfred's features to its single user. It may be processed by an AI service provider for that purpose.

Sharing. Data is never sold or shared for advertising, and is not used to train AI models.

Storage. Data is stored on a private, secured server.

Deletion. Access can be removed at any time from the Google account's third-party access page. Stored data is deleted on request.

Google API Services. Alfred's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact. Via the support email shown on Alfred's Google sign-in screen.
